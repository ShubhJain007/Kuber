Official SIMSHIFT heatsink split (source fins 5–8 → zero-shot on unseen OOD fins 10–12), paper's metrics, lower is better. The published baselines adapt to the target domain (UDA) using target data — so they are not zero-shot; the paper also reports only their temperature + velocity (no pressure, mean-nRMSE, or params → "n/r"). Kuber Nano trains from scratch on the source split and is evaluated zero-shot, predicting the full 5-channel field — beating the previous best on temperature (12.14 vs 12.41 K) and setting SOTA velocity (0.038 vs 0.039 m/s) on geometries it never trained on. Kuber Nano figures are the best across its geometry-encoder configs (SDF / surface).
